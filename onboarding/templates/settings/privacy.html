What ORBIT stores, and where

This deployment keeps everything on its own disk. Nothing about you goes to an analytics product, and nothing is sold or used to train anything.

The audit log, specifically

ORBIT records what it did: event names, ids, timings, which model answered, and whether a tool call was approved. It does not record the text of your messages or replies.

The Analytics screen is built by reading that same log — no new tracking was added to produce it. If you want a log with less in it, the honest answer is that trimming it is not built yet; deleting audit.log resets the view and ORBIT starts a new one.

Take your data with you

One JSON file: your account record, your preferences, every project with its listing, your feedback, and your session history. Credential material — the password hash and any live verification or reset token — is deliberately left out of the file.

Export everything (.json) Downloads immediately. Nothing is queued or emailed.
