What happens next

  1. 1 Your account existsName, email, password. Nothing else is asked for.
  2. 2 You confirm the email Google and GitHub sign-ups skip this — the provider already vouched for the address.
  3. 3 You choose a starting point New project, import, connect Git, or just talk to it. Skippable, all four.

What we ask for, and why

  • Required: a name to greet you by, an email to reach you at, a password.
  • Optional: the workspace name, and every personalization question later — all of it skippable, all of it editable.
  • Never: a card. ORBIT is free while it is in beta and there is no payment form anywhere in the product.

About the provider buttons

Google sign-in asks for openid email profile. GitHub asks for read:user user:email. Neither asks for your repositories, and neither can read your code because of it.

Google and GitHub accounts skip the confirmation step — the provider has already verified the address. Email accounts confirm it before the workspace opens.
