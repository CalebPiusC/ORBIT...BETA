{# ============ Privacy & security ============ #}

Privacy & security

What's stored, and the locks on your account.

Your privacy, in plain terms

  • Conversations, projects and feedback live in this workspace's local store — not on an ORBIT server.
  • Voice clips are processed locally for transcription and discarded; they are never stored.
  • When you talk to a model, the request goes to your configured provider (Gemini, or your OpenRouter chain) and nowhere else.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed (PBKDF2); verification codes expire after ten minutes.
